## Audience and address

- Who listens, and in what window?
- One listener or a group? (If more than one, narrate in the third person and never address one person.)

## How to handle sources

- Every claim names its source; anything without one is cut.
- Each clip gets a short spoken intro saying why it matters to the listener's lens.
- If a clip cannot be aligned, narrate the point and link to the episode at the timestamp.

## Plain language

- Explain any technical term in one sentence the first time it comes up.
